# Prospective-user interview guide

- **Status:** **NOT DONE.** The project lead must run 3–5 interviews (PRD §2.1).
- Use public or synthetic examples only. Do not collect employer-confidential material.

## Who to recruit

Technical or business analysts, engineering managers or architects who have written a comparison
or decision brief in the last three months. Aim for at least one person in the reviewer role.

## Script (about 30 minutes)

1. **Context (5 min).** "Tell me about the last research or decision brief you produced. Who
   asked for it, and who read it?"
2. **Walk-through (10 min).** "Walk me through how you produced it, step by step." Probe for:
   - how they broke the question down,
   - where they searched,
   - how they recorded sources,
   - what they did when sources disagreed,
   - how long each step took (estimates are fine; record them as estimates).
3. **Bottlenecks (5 min).** "Which step was slowest or most error-prone? What did a reviewer
   push back on?"
4. **Trust (5 min).** Show the synthetic example from the [project brief](project-brief.md).
   Ask:
   - "Would you accept this? What would you check first?"
   - "What would make you distrust it?"
5. **Approval (3 min).** "Who would need to approve a plan or a final report? Would approving a
   plan before research starts help or get in the way?"
6. **Wrap-up (2 min).** "If this saved you nothing, what would it still need to do to be worth
   using?"

## Decision rule

If fewer than 3 of 5 interviewees name evidence gathering, source reconciliation or citation
checking as a real bottleneck, revisit the problem statement and domain before Week 3.
